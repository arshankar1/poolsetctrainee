First impression forms in 7 seconds. Do SEC every time.

3 Things Customers Want

  • Reliability — Show up as promised
  • Trust — Be honest and transparent
  • Communication — Keep them informed proactively

The goal is raving fans — not just satisfied customers.

Complaint Handling — LAUA

1 Listen — let them finish, don't interrupt
2 Acknowledge — "I hear you and understand"
3 Understand — ask clarifying questions
4 Act — tell them what you'll do, do it

⛔ Never defend yourself before acknowledging.

Satisfied vs. Raving Fan

Satisfied: Pays, stays quiet, may cancel

Raving Fan:

  • Refers friends & neighbors
  • Leaves 5-star reviews
  • Defends you when things go wrong
  • Stays through price increases

Proactive Service — The Raving Fan Creator

Notice and communicate issues before the customer mentions them. "I noticed the heater is making a faint sound — nothing alarming yet, but I'm monitoring it." This single habit separates great technicians from average ones.
