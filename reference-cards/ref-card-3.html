8-Step Service Visit Checklist

  • 1 Scan — check pool & yard for safety issues or damage
  • 2 Test water — pH, alk, chlorine, CYA (record readings)
  • 3 Skim — remove floating leaves and surface debris
  • 4 Brush — walls, steps, and floor edges (correct brush type!)
  • 5 Vacuum — floor, slow overlapping passes (25–30% overlap)
  • 6 Clean skimmer basket — empty & rinse
  • 7 Add chemicals — based on test results (AFTER all cleaning)
  • 8 Check equipment — pump, filter pressure, timeclock, note issues

Brush Selection

  • Plaster/Gunite: Combo nylon + SS steel
  • Vinyl liner: Soft nylon ONLY
  • Fiberglass: Soft nylon ONLY

⛔ Never use steel on vinyl or fiberglass

Vacuuming Tips

  • Move slowly — don't stir up sediment
  • 25–30% overlap on each pass
  • Straight passes end to end
  • Start in deep end → shallow end
  • Watch pressure gauge during vacuuming

⚠️ Why Chemicals Go LAST

Adding chemicals before or during cleaning stirs debris that absorbs chemicals unevenly. Always complete ALL mechanical cleaning first, then add chemicals to a settled pool for proper distribution.
