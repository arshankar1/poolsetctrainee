  • Drink water every 15 minutes
  • Danger threshold: 100.4°F body temp
  • Wear light-colored clothing and hat
  • Most deaths occur in first few days

Heat Illness Symptoms

Watch for:

  • Headache · Nausea · Dizziness
  • Weakness · Confusion
  • Heavy sweating · Irritability

Heat Stroke (CALL 911): Confusion, no sweating, temp above 104°F, loss of consciousness

Smith System — 5 Keys

  • 1. Aim High: Look 15–20 sec ahead
  • 2. Big Picture: Scan all mirrors & sides
  • 3. Eyes Moving: Shift gaze every 2 sec
  • 4. Leave an Out: Keep escape route open
  • 5. Be Seen: Lights, signals, horn
