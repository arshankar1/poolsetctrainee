  • Chemistry — pH, alk, chlorine, CYA

"pH is mood, alkalinity is the bodyguard."

CYA (Cyanuric Acid)

  • Protects chlorine from UV sunlight
  • Ideal: 30–50 ppm
  • Over-stabilization: above 80–100 ppm
  • Fix: Drain & refill — no chemical removes CYA
  • CYA accumulates with trichlor tabs

Pump Runtime Guidelines

  • Summer (hot weather): 8–12 hours/day
  • Cool/off-season: 6–8 hours/day
  • Goal: at least 1 full pool volume turnover per day
  • Verify timeclock setting at every visit
