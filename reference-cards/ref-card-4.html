🚨 THE BIG RED RULE — SHOCK SAFETY

NEVER add granular shock directly to the pool, skimmer, or any concentrated form.

Always pre-dissolve shock in a bucket of water first (add chemical to water — not water to chemical). Distribute solution around pool perimeter.

CYA Guide

  • Ideal outdoor pool: 30–50 ppm
  • Over-stabilized: above 80–100 ppm
  • At 150 ppm: chlorine barely works
  • Fix: Drain & refill only
  • No chemical removes CYA
  • CYA adds up from trichlor tabs

⛔ Never Mix

  • Shock + Muriatic Acid
    Creates toxic chlorine gas
  • Always wait 30+ min between shock and acid additions
  • Never add acid to skimmer
  • Never mix chemicals in same bucket

Phosphates

Phosphates are algae's primary food source. Act when above 200 ppb. Use phosphate remover — water may turn milky briefly, this is normal. High phosphates = persistent algae despite correct chemistry.
