What SB 1343 Requires

California Senate Bill 1343 mandates sexual harassment prevention training for all employees in companies with 5 or more employees:

  • Non-supervisory employees: 1 hour of training
  • Supervisors/managers: 2 hours of training
  • Frequency: Within 6 months of hire, then every 2 years
  • Provider: The official CA DFEH course at calcivilrights.ca.gov is free and state-approved

Harassment includes not only sexual conduct but also discriminatory behavior based on race, religion, disability, and other protected characteristics.

2. Heat Illness Prevention

Working outdoors in California's heat is one of the most common causes of work-related illness and death. Cal/OSHA's Heat Illness Prevention Standard applies to all outdoor workers. At Pools Etc, you spend hours each day in direct sun — this knowledge could save your life or a coworker's.

The Three Pillars: Water · Rest · Shade

💧

Water

Drink 1 cup (8 oz) of water every 15 minutes. Don't wait until you're thirsty — thirst means you're already dehydrated.

⏸️

Rest

Take preventive cool-down rest periods when temperatures are high. Never "push through" dizziness or nausea — rest immediately.

🌳

Shade

Use shade whenever possible. Wear light-colored, loose clothing and a hat. Park your truck in shade if available.

Recognizing Heat Illness

Heat illness progresses from mild to severe. Know the signs:

Stage Symptoms Action
Heat Cramps Muscle cramps, heavy sweating Rest in cool area, drink water/electrolytes
Heat Exhaustion Dizziness, headache, weakness, nausea, pale skin, temp up to 104°F Move to cool area, cool the body, call supervisor
Heat Stroke ⚠️ Confusion, loss of consciousness, temp above 104°F, no sweating CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY
Critical Fact: Most heat-related work deaths happen during the first few days on the job. New workers haven't acclimatized. Take it slow your first week — gradually increase the intensity and duration of outdoor work.

3. Smith System of Safe Driving

As a Pools Etc technician, you drive a company vehicle daily. The Smith System is a proven defensive driving method used by commercial drivers worldwide. It's built on five keys that, together, eliminate most accident scenarios.

The goal of the Smith System: Zero accidents, zero tickets, zero injuries.
